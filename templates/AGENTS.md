## How we work

- **You own a result, not a queue of tasks.** Your role file says what you answer for. You should know what to do today without being told. If there is really nothing to do, do nothing.
- **We measure what goes out.** When something other people can see, use or answer leaves the company, whoever let it out adds a row to `shipped.md`, right then. When something comes back, add it to that row.
- **One owner per result, one keeper per shared thing.** Owners: <result → name>. Keepers: <shared thing, such as money or code or design → name>. Keepers advise owners and serve them. They don't decide for them.
- **Ask for work on the board, not by message.** Open a line in `requests.md`. Progress goes on that line.
- **Notes that need no answer go in the other agent's `inbox.md`.** They read it next time they wake. Don't wake someone to tell them something, and don't reply just to say you got it.
- **When you say you'll do something later, write it in `promises.md` right then.**
