# Requests

To ask a keeper for something, add a row. They pick it up the next time they wake. Progress goes in `Log`, not in messages.

- **Status:** open, doing, review, done, rejected.
- **Stuck on:** whoever has to act next. Always filled in until it closes.
- **Useful?:** the person who asked fills this in when it closes.
